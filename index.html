Summary of Malaysia Weather Data - 2022

A comparative analysis of key meteorological trends across states.

Air Pollution Level
This map visualizes the mean air pollution levels across various monitoring stations in Malaysia for the year 2022. Each circle represents a station, with the size constant indicating the pollution level based on the recorded values. Bigger size correspond to higher pollution levels, allowing for quick identification of areas
with elevated air quality concerns. Users can hover over each station to see the exact location and pollution value, providing both a spatial overview and detailed data insights. This visualization helps policymakers and the public understand regional variations in air quality and identify hotspots that require attention.
Temperature
The chart above presents the average temperature by month and state across Malaysia. Each colored line represents a different state, allowing for comparison of temperature trends throughout the year. Overall, the average temperature remains relatively consistent, fluctuating between reflecting Malaysia’s stable tropical climate. A slight increase in temperature is observed from January to April, followed by a modest decline during the mid-year months (May to September).
The red dashed line marks the Southwest Monsoon Transition, which typically brings warmer and drier conditions from March onward. Despite minor regional variations—such as slightly higher temperatures in inland states like Kedah and Kuala Lumpur compared to coastal or highland regions—the overall trend indicates minimal temperature variation nationwide. The dropdown menu labeled “Select a State” allows users to focus on specific states for closer examination.
